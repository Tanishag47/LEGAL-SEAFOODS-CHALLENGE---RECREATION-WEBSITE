OUR STORY →

Founded in 1950 as a family fish market just outside of Boston,
we are woven into the fabric and history of New England.
With pride we serve our comminity, and beyond.

  • New England Bred: rooted in the region’s rich fishing traditions.
  • We're built on quality: with a commitment to the freshest, highest-standard seafood.
  • From a local brand to a global presence: while staying true to our New England identity.
  • Freshness is our promise: from the quality of the catch, to the deliciousness of the meal.
  • Focused on sustainability and innovation: envisioning a better future for oceans and communities.
  • Best of the catch: we prioritize only serving the best of the best, caught daily here in New England.

New England Classics served right on the Boston Harbor

Baked Stuffed Lobster

A Tourist favorite, this dish is seeked by hungry people globally.
An almost 2lb lobster - baked to perfection - stuffed with shrimp, scallops and Ritz cracker buttery goodness.

New England Clam Chowder

Our classic award winning recipe. Our thick and creamy clam chowder is packed with tender clams, hearty potatoes, salted pork and onions. Served at almost every presidention inauguration since 1981.

Cod Salt + Vinegar

Crispy Icelandic-style cod served with golden potato wedges, smoky napa cabbage and bacon slaw, and a tangy remoulade for a bold, balanced finish.

Shellfish Tower + Raw Bar

With our commitment to freshness, our shellfish tower comes with the best pick of local oysters, clams, little necks, chilled lobster tails and more.
